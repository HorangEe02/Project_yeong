# AJIN Employee Benefits Guide (HR Affairs Team)

This document summarizes the in-house benefits available to new and existing employees. New or changed items are announced first by the HR Affairs team.

## 1. Leave Policy

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Annual leave | Less than 1 year of service: 1 day per month (up to 11 days). 1+ year: 15 days plus seniority bonus. Fiscal-year basis. |
| Half-day leave | Morning (09–13) / Afternoon (14–18). Counts as 0.5 day. |
| Bereavement / events | Marriage 5 days / Childbirth 10 days / Parent or spouse passing 5 days / Sibling passing 3 days |
| Maternity / parental | Statutory 90 days + company supplement. Parental leave up to 1 year (both parents). |
| Carry-over | Unused annual leave can be carried into the next fiscal year through June. Anything remaining is forfeited. |

## 2. Insurance & Medical

- **4 social insurances**: Health, National Pension, Employment, Industrial Accident — 100% enrolled
- **Group accident insurance**: Self + spouse + children (company-paid)
- **Annual health checkup**: 1x per year (self) + biennial spouse checkup
- **Medical reimbursement**: 50% of self-pay portion for inpatient care (annual cap KRW 2,000,000)

## 3. Meals & Commute

- **In-house cafeteria**: Free at HQ and plants. Breakfast / lunch / dinner (dinner for night shift only)
- **Commuter bus**: 6 main routes (Gyeongsan↔Daegu, Gyeongsan↔Yeongcheon, etc.) — KRW 1,000 each way
- **Travel meal allowance**: Domestic KRW 30,000 / day, overseas USD 80 / day

## 4. Family & Children

- **Tuition support**: High school / university tuition for employees with 5+ years of service (cap KRW 12,000,000 / year)
- **Daycare / preschool support**: KRW 300,000 / month (children under 7)
- **Club activity grants**: Company subsidy + quarterly activity stipend of KRW 300,000

## 5. Housing & Self-development

- **Company apartment**: Lease for newly married / single employees (80% of deposit, interest-free)
- **Self-development budget**: KRW 1,000,000 / year (language, certifications, books)
- **In-house library**: 10,000+ volumes on HQ 1F, e-book subscriptions (Riss, KISS)

## 6. Recreation

- **Company condominiums**: 4 sites (Gangwon, Jeju, Yeosu) — reservation-based, KRW 50,000 / night
- **Holiday gifts**: Seollal, Chuseok, and Foundation Day (8/24) — 3 times per year
- **Athletic facilities**: HQ gym and sauna free of charge; Cheonan / Gwangju plants have separate facilities
- **Employee overseas trip**: Biennial company-supported trip

## 7. How to Apply

Please contact the HR Affairs team via the internal intranet for application channels and processing.
