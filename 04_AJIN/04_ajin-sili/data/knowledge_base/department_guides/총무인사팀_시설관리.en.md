# AJIN Facilities Management Guide (HR Affairs Team)

Reference for daily facility usage, maintenance requests, and badge access.

## 1. Badge / Access Control

- All employees receive an RFID badge on the first day. Loss requires immediate report to HR Affairs.
- Restricted zones (R&D lab, mold storage, server room) require additional access approval.

## 2. Common Areas

- **Meeting rooms**: Reserve through the internal portal (intranet → Facilities → Meeting rooms)
- **Cafeteria**: Open 07:00 – 21:00. Night-shift meals require advance entry.
- **Parking**: Free for employees. Visitors should register in advance via security.

## 3. Maintenance Requests

- **Routine issues** (lighting, HVAC, plumbing): Submit through the intranet maintenance ticket form
- **Urgent issues**: Call HR Affairs ext. 1100 or use the 24h emergency line at the security desk
- **IT-related issues**: Routed to the IT Strategy team (separate ticket queue)

## 4. Health & Safety

- Emergency assembly points are posted on every floor.
- AEDs (defibrillators) are located on HQ 1F, 3F, and the cafeteria entrance.
- Smoking is permitted only in designated outdoor areas.

## 5. Contact

For facility-related inquiries, please contact the HR Affairs team via intranet or extension 1100.
