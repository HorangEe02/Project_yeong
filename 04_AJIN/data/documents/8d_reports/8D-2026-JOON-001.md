## D0 — Awareness / Emergency Response
- 2026-02-10: HMGMA SQ team reported abnormal noise from EWP units installed in Ioniq 5 pilot production.
- Affected lot: LOT-EWP-0210 (150 units)
- Immediate containment: 100% inspection of remaining stock at HMGMA line-side.

## D2 — Problem Description
- Symptom: Abnormal vibration and noise from EWP during thermal cycling test.
- Frequency: 8 out of 150 units (5.3% defect rate)
- Location: Impeller-motor coupling area
- Customer Impact: Potential cooling system performance degradation

## D3 — Interim Containment Action (ICA)
- 100% vibration test added at end-of-line for all EWP assemblies
- Suspect lot (LOT-EWP-0210~0215) quarantined: 420 units
- Rework/replace impeller coupling on all quarantined units

## D4 — Root Cause Analysis
- **Root Cause**: Impeller press-fit depth inconsistent due to fixture alignment deviation (0.15mm shift)
- **Contributing Factor**: New fixture calibration procedure not documented for night shift
- Verification: Cpk of press-fit depth was 0.89 (below 1.33 requirement)

## D5 — Permanent Corrective Action (PCA)
1. Fixture re-calibrated and alignment verified with laser measurement
2. Press-fit depth monitoring sensor installed (real-time SPC)
3. Cpk improved to 1.67 after correction
4. Night shift fixture calibration checklist added to SOP

## D6 — Implementation & Validation
- PCA implemented: 2026-02-20
- Validation: 500 units produced with zero defects
- HMGMA SQ approval: 2026-02-25

## D7 — Prevention
- Fixture calibration schedule: every shift change (3x/day)
- Annual fixture replacement plan established
- Lesson learned shared with Alabama plant (AJIN USA)

- Team acknowledged by JOON INC Plant Manager
