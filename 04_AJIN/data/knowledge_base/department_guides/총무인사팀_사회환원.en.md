# AJIN Corporate Social Contribution Guide (HR Affairs Team)

This document outlines AJIN Industry's corporate social-contribution programs and employee participation channels.

## 1. Core Pillars

- **Community partnerships** — sister-village relationships with Gyeongsan / Yeongcheon agricultural villages
- **Environmental stewardship** — annual tree-planting day, plant-wide single-use-plastic reduction
- **Education sponsorship** — scholarships and mentorship for nearby technical high schools
- **Disaster relief** — corporate matching donations triggered by natural disasters

## 2. Employee Volunteering

- **Volunteer leave**: Up to 16 hours per year, treated as paid leave with prior approval
- **Talent donations**: Engineering mentorship, English tutoring, and CAD workshops for partner schools
- **Donation matching**: 1:1 corporate match up to KRW 500,000 per employee per year

## 3. ESG Reporting

- All social-contribution activities are aggregated into the annual ESG report
- Quarterly newsletter announces highlight programs

## 4. How to Participate

Visit the internal intranet → HR Affairs → Social Contribution → Sign up for the desired program, or contact the HR Affairs team directly.
