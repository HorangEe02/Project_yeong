# AJIN Recruitment & Hiring Guide (HR Affairs Team)

Reference for recruitment programs, internal referrals, and onboarding flow at AJIN Industry.

## 1. Recruitment Tracks

- **New graduate recruitment** — annual cycle, typically September–November. Major areas: production engineering, R&D, QA, business support.
- **Experienced hire** — open year-round through the careers page; reviewed weekly.
- **Co-op / Internship** — partnerships with technical high schools and universities.

## 2. Selection Process

1. Application screening
2. Aptitude test (online)
3. Technical / engineering interview
4. Executive interview
5. Health check + reference verification
6. Offer

The cycle typically takes 4–6 weeks. Updates are sent by email.

## 3. Internal Referral Program

- Employees may refer external candidates. If hired and retained for 6 months, the referring employee receives a bonus (KRW 500,000–1,000,000 depending on level).
- Submit referrals through the intranet → HR Affairs → Internal referral form.

## 4. Onboarding

- **Day 1**: Badge issuance, IT account setup, security briefing
- **Week 1**: General orientation (mandatory)
- **Month 1**: Department-specific OJT plan owned by the team lead
- **Month 3**: Probationary evaluation; full conversion follows

## 5. Contact

For recruitment status, internal referrals, or onboarding questions, contact the HR Affairs team via intranet.
